OUR MISSION & PHILOSOPHY

About ABNF

Abandoned but Not Forgotten (ABNF) is an interactive deep-space archive honoring the robotic pioneers that ventured into the cosmic unknown on behalf of all humanity.

Why Remember?

When space missions conclude, headlines fade. Yet their artifacts remain: rovers perched on martian ridge-lines, lunar rovers parked beside pristine craters, and probes coasting forever through the dark between stars. ABNF exists to ensure their discoveries, courage, and poetry remain vivid, accessible, and inspiring for the next generation of explorers.

Open Science & Public Data Sources

  • NASA JPL Horizons API: Real-time orbital ephemerides and distance telemetry for Voyager 1 and planetary bodies.
  • NASA Open APIs & NASA Image Library: Historical mission photography, rover imagery, and APOD.
  • NASA Trek WMTS: High-resolution lunar and martian surface cartography tiles.
  • Google Gemini AI: Historical persona simulation engines emulating spacecraft telemetry logs.
  • Supabase Auth & Database: User profiles and community memorial tributes.
