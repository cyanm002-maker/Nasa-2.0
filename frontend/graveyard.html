THE SCIENCE OF DISPOSAL

Planetary Protection Protocols

Under COSPAR international treaties, spacecraft that might harbor Earth microbes must not contaminate habitable worlds like Europa or Enceladus. This is why Galileo and Cassini were commanded into suicidal plunges into Jupiter and Saturn.

INTERSTELLAR AMBASSADORS

The Five Escape Probes

Only five human objects possess enough hyperbolic excess velocity to escape the Sun’s gravity well forever: Voyager 1, Voyager 2, Pioneer 10, Pioneer 11, and New Horizons. They will outlast the Earth and Moon.
